# Cocos Capital

> Cocos Capital is an Argentine investech and online broker (ALyC, founded 2021 in Buenos Aires) offering a 100% digital investment platform for retail investors. Users trade Argentine stocks, CEDEARs, sovereign and corporate bonds, mutual funds, the MEP dollar, US stocks via an international account, and crypto. Cocos is regulated by Argentina's Comision Nacional de Valores (CNV), settles through the BYMA group, and has surpassed 300,000 accounts.
